Before you write

Can you quote a price?

Not yet, and any number quoted before a yard is appointed would be fiction. The program page sets out the cost structure so you can see what drives it.

Will it fit our berth?

Beam overall is 22.4 m, draft 3.5–3.8 m depending on configuration, length 70.6 m. Those three numbers answer most berth questions immediately, and they rule the vessel out more often than anything else.

Is it certified?

No. No classification society has been appointed and no plans have been submitted. The intended route is set out on the compliance page.
