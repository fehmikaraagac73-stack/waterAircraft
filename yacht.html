Yacht / Luxury Watercraft

747 Luxury Yacht. Up to 30 guests in ten to twelve en-suite cabins, hybrid electric propulsion, a 400 m² solar array on the crown, and an open two-level sun deck where the aft fuselage is cut away.

SOLAS USCG 46 CFR SUBCH. T LARGE YACHT CODE HYBRID ELECTRIC

Description

One of one, and unmistakably so from three miles away.

The yacht configuration has the highest margin per hull and the smallest number of people to evacuate. It is also the one where the airframe's provenance becomes the story.

A 70-metre yacht from a conventional yard is a known quantity: known yards, known waiting lists, known silhouette. This is not that. The buyer for this vessel is specifically buying a hull nobody else can order, with a documented former life that can be traced by tail number.

Forward, under the retained 747 upper deck, sits the main salon and formal dining, with the overhead opened to the crown and the original window stations enlarged into a continuous glazed band. The owner's suite occupies the full beam of the fuselage immediately aft of the upper deck stair, with guest cabins running aft from there — ten to twelve in total, all en-suite, each on the outboard wall with its own window line.

The aft third of the fuselage crown is removed entirely. That produces a two-level open sun deck with a pool and jacuzzi on the upper level, sheltered lounge below, and a direct stair down to the swim platform between the pontoon sterns. A tender garage is built into the starboard pontoon with a side-opening door.

Propulsion is hybrid electric — 2 × 1,800 kW with battery support, so the vessel can lie at anchor overnight or manoeuvre in a marina on electric power alone. The 400 m² of solar on the fuselage crown covers a meaningful share of hotel load at anchor in good conditions; it does not propel the vessel, and we do not claim it does.

Use cases

Where this configuration earns.

Private ownership

A fully custom, one-of-one vessel with a sustainability narrative that survives scrutiny — the airframe would otherwise have been scrapped, and that is a checkable fact.

VIP and corporate charter

Thirty guests, a 400 m² deck and a silhouette that photographs from any angle. Charter rates on unusual hulls hold better through the shoulder season.

Resort flagship

Moored as the centrepiece of a property, operating day charters and evening events, with hotel load carried on shore power and solar rather than generators.

Expedition-style cruising

Shallow 3.5 m draft and a wide stable platform suit anchorages that a deep monohull of the same length cannot reach.

Key features

Yacht configuration.

01Luxury interiors

02Hybrid propulsion

2 × 1,800 kW with battery support for silent manoeuvring and overnight lying at anchor.

400 m² on the fuselage crown, sized against hotel load rather than propulsion.

Fin or gyro stabilisation as an owner option, on top of the inherent twin-hull stability.
